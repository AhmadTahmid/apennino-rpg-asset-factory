# Blind visual audit

The reviewer used only the anonymous A–D review sheet and anonymous edge
overlays. Candidate identities and provenance were hidden until after the
verdict.

## Ranking

`C > A > D > B`

### Candidate C

Strongest aesthetic result. It reads as a cohesive pixel-painterly Appennino
JRPG scene with consistent masonry, roofs, foliage, mountain depth, useful
player-scale characters, and a legible plaza/stair/door network. It is dense
and occasionally overdecorated, but it feels authored rather than collaged.

### Candidate A

Excellent visual alternate: beautiful palette, strong fountain composition,
coherent elevation changes, and integrated lighting/materials. Uniform detail
weakens focal hierarchy, while cropped roofs and busy bridge/terrace junctions
reduce navigation clarity.

### Candidate D

Attractive local rendering, but weaker world logic. The upper terrace reads as
a floating or unsupported slab, upper/lower connections are unclear, and dark
alleys are ambiguous. It feels more like a nice illustration than a confident
playable map.

### Candidate B

Weakest of the four. Materials are cohesive, but the upper promenade appears
to float over a void, the lower plaza is cramped, the fountain is weakly
staged, and large paved areas contain little navigation information.

## Blind recommendation

The visual reviewer recommended C for downstream experimentation and A as the
alternate. This recommendation considered appearance and apparent navigation
only; it did not assess preservation of the supplied structural scaffold.
