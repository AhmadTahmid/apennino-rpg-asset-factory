# Moonroot Hollow — Visual Audit

## Verdict

**Pass for a lean complete prototype.** The scene is attractive, the gameplay
surface is immediately readable, and the 2× traveler scale corrects the most
obvious portability residual from Experiment F.

## Observations

- The clearing reads as one elevation with generous lanes around the well.
- Mora, the moonwell, and the gatekeeper are visually distinct and ordered
  left-to-right well enough for a three-stage quest.
- The traveler is smaller than the tall gatekeeper but plausible as a human
  beside a large antlered figure. It no longer reads miniature against paving.
- Blue-violet values are dense, yet the traveler's warm skin, gray coat, and
  brown boots remain distinguishable.
- The lower foliage frames the scene without hiding the declared spawn or test
  route.
- The giant-tree doorway and side lanes look suggestive but remain blocked
  scenery in this one-screen scope.
- Baked NPCs create no causal animation or depth claim; interaction feedback is
  text and quest state only.

## Constraint audit

The final visible illustration consists only of generated/reused raster PNGs.
No SVG or procedural primitive artwork is present. Runtime text is rendered by
Godot; invisible collision shapes are gameplay data.
